About Me

Infrastructure, automation, and reliability focused.

Berke Muftuoglu is a Data Automation Engineer at Nossa Data (a sustainability-focused data platform processing ESG reports at scale) with a software engineering background and hands-on DevOps experience. He specializes in cloud platforms (AWS, Azure), CI/CD pipelines, and Infrastructure as Code using Terraform and Ansible. His technical foundation includes an MSc in Emerging Digital Technologies from University College London (Merit) and a BSc in Computer Science from King's College London (First-Class Honours). He values performance, reliability, and maintainable systems, with a focus on test-driven development and quantifiable metrics.

His professional experience spans data automation, DevOps engineering, and distributed systems research. At Nossa Data, he leads data automation initiatives focusing on reliable workflows and maintainable automation of ESG data processing pipelines using Python and TypeScript. Previously, at BtcTurk (Turkey's largest cryptocurrency exchange), he built Infrastructure as Code with Terraform and Ansible, configured CI/CD pipelines via Azure DevOps, and implemented Kubernetes backup and disaster recovery with Velero. He also conducted research on Federated Learning within Zero Trust frameworks at NetraScale (a UCL Industry Exchange Network partner) and contributed to .NET 6 microservices at Getir (rapid grocery delivery platform).

Berke is actively seeking DevOps, Platform Engineering, and Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) roles where he can improve delivery speed, system resilience, and operational clarity. His expertise includes Python, Bash, Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform, AWS, Azure DevOps, Git, CI/CD automation, Linux, and monitoring with Datadog. He is tool-agnostic and language-agnostic, focusing on solving infrastructure and automation problems with the right technology for each context.
